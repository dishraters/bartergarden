# How to Build Habits That Actually Stick

We've all been there — January starts with grand resolutions, but by February, most of us are back to our old ways. The truth is, building lasting habits isn't about willpower. It's about strategy.

## Start Smaller Than You Think

The biggest mistake people make? Starting too big. Want to exercise? Don't commit to an hour at the gym. Start with 5 minutes. Want to read more? Start with one page.

> "Success is the sum of small efforts, repeated day in and day out." — Robert Collier

### The Two-Minute Rule

If something takes less than two minutes to do, do it immediately. This works because:

1. It removes the friction of "deciding" to do something
2. It builds momentum
3. It creates a consistent identity

## Make It Obvious

Your environment shapes your behavior more than you realize. Try these tricks:

- **Habit stacking**: Attach new habits to existing ones
- **Environment design**: Put running shoes by the door
- **Visual cues**: Keep your book on your pillow

## The Power of Consistency Over Intensity

Here's a surprising truth: it doesn't matter how much you do. What matters is that you do it consistently.

| Approach | Result |
|----------|--------|
| 20 min/day, every day | ✅ Builds lasting habit |
| 2 hours once a week | ❌ Easy to skip |
